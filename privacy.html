ExpiryPal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ExpiryPal helps you track expiry dates. It is built to work on your phone without an account, and it does not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Data stored on your device

The items you add (names, brands, categories, expiry and opening dates, locations, reminder settings and photos) and your settings are stored on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device (see iCloud backup below for the copy kept on iPhone). If you export a backup, the app writes a file of your items, places and reminder settings, without photos, and you choose where to save or send it. The app does not upload that file.

iCloud backup (iPhone)

On iPhone, the app keeps a copy of your items, places and reminder settings, without photos, in your own private iCloud storage (the app's iCloud container, tied to your Apple ID). It is used to offer restoring your list when you install the app again or set up a new iPhone with the same Apple ID. The copy is stored by Apple under your iCloud account; we cannot see or access it. If iCloud is turned off for the app, no copy is kept. You can delete it in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Free Pro trial

New installs get Pro for free for a limited time. To know when the trial ends, the app stores the date it was first opened on your device, in the iOS Keychain on iPhone so that it survives reinstalling the app, and includes it in backup files you export. With the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID described under Purchases, the app also sends the trial start date, its length and whether it is active, ended or followed by a purchase, so we can see how the trial works. This is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read printed expiry dates and to take item photos. Date recognition runs entirely on your device. Photos you take or pick from your library stay on your device.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Purchases

ExpiryPal Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives a random, anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store. This data is used to unlock Pro and is not linked to your name or email.

With the same anonymous ID, the app also sends a few app details to RevenueCat: the answers you choose during onboarding (such as what you want to track), the category of the first item you add, and the trial details described above. We use them to understand which features matter and to improve the app. Item names, dates and photos are never sent.

Barcode lookup

If you scan a barcode to fill in a product name, the barcode number (and nothing else about you or your list) is sent to public product databases, Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb, to look up the name. Like any web request, these services see your IP address. Dates are always read on the device.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app does not show third-party ads, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Creator codes and install source

If you redeem a creator's offer code, install the app on Android from a creator's link, or entered a creator code in an earlier version, the app remembers which creator referred you. That creator's short name is stored with the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID described above, so we can pay creators a share of the purchases they bring in. On iPhone the app reads the name of the redeemed offer from your App Store purchase. On Android the creator's name is read once from Google Play's install referrer. It is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

When you open a creator's link on this website, we count the visit for that creator with GoatCounter, without cookies and without storing your IP address, so we can see how many people each creator sends.

Children

ExpiryPal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app adds features such as ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
