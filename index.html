FAQ

Good questions

Is ExpiryPal really free?

Yes. You can track up to 10 items for free, with date scanning, open-jar dates, barcode lookup and reminders. Pro removes the limit and adds photos, places, category reminders, backup and app icons.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Dates are read on your phone and reminders are scheduled on your phone, so neither needs the internet. Only looking up a product name from its barcode needs a connection.

What happens when I change phones?

Your items live on your phone, not in an account. With Pro you can back up your items to a file and bring them to your new phone. Restore Pro itself from Settings › Restore purchases.

Does it sync between my devices?

No. There is no account and nothing is stored on a server, so each phone keeps its own list. That is what keeps your data private.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, ExpiryPal is on the App Store and Google Play.

What do 6M and 12M mean on cosmetics?

It is the period-after-opening symbol: a small open jar with a number of months. 12M means use it within 12 months of opening. Pick the symbol in ExpiryPal, say when you opened it, and it works out the date.
