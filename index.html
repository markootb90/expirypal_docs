FAQ

Good questions

Is ExpiryPal really free?

Yes. Every new install gets all of Pro free for 14 days. No card, nothing to cancel. After that, ExpiryPal stays free for up to 10 items, and everything you added keeps reminding you.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, dates are read on the device, and we never see your items or photos. The app has no ad or analytics trackers. To run subscriptions it shares an anonymous ID and a few app details with RevenueCat, and a barcode you scan is looked up in public product databases. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Dates are read on your phone and reminders are scheduled on your phone, so neither needs the internet. Only looking up a product name from its barcode needs a connection.

What happens when I change phones?

On iPhone, ExpiryPal keeps a copy of your list (without photos) in your own iCloud and offers to restore it the first time you open the app on a new iPhone. On any phone, Pro can also export your items to a file. Restore Pro itself from Settings › Restore purchases.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not live. There is no account, so each phone keeps its own list. On iPhone, the iCloud copy is used to bring your list back on a new phone, not to sync two phones at once.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, ExpiryPal is on the App Store and Google Play.

What do 6M and 12M mean on cosmetics?

It is the period-after-opening symbol: a small open jar with a number of months. 12M means use it within 12 months of opening. Pick the symbol in ExpiryPal, say when you opened it, and it works out the date.
